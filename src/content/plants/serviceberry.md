One of the best native berries and planted everywhere as an ornamental — but it ripens **late June to early July**, so by late July you've likely missed this year's window. The move now: **note the bushes** and come back next June.

There's already one on the map near the Verdun riverfront.

## Harvest
- Ripe berries are deep purple and soft, and come off with a gentle roll of the fingers.
- Birds love them — a well-placed bush can be stripped in a day when ripe, so check daily as they turn.

## Prep
- Eat fresh; flavour is like a sweeter, almondy blueberry.
- Classic in pies, muffins, pancakes, and jam (the prairie "saskatoon pie"). Freeze exactly like blueberries.

## Notes
_(Mark bloom date and ripening date each year — that's how you catch the window.)_
