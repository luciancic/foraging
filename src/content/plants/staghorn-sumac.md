The tart red spice-and-lemonade plant, ripening in **August**. Flag the colonies now (they're easy to spot) and come back when the cones deepen to firm red.

## Harvest
- Pick whole cones on a **dry day** (rain washes out the tart coating that gives the flavour). Ripe cones are deep red and firm.
- Taste-test: lick a cone — it should be distinctly sour/tart. Bland means under-ripe or rain-washed.

## Prep
- **Sumac-ade:** crush a few cones, soak in **cold** water 15–30 min (hot water pulls out bitter tannins), swish, strain through cloth, sweeten. A pink, tart, lemonade-like drink.
- **Spice:** dry the berries, rub off and grind the outer red flesh from the seeds — that red powder is the tangy *za'atar* sumac. Great on flatbread, fish, salads.

## Notes
_(Mark the colonies now; check ripeness through August.)_
