The easiest, safest wild berry there is — if it looks like a raspberry, it is one. Grazing on the spot is the whole point; anything past that is a bonus.

## Harvest
- Pick when **fully red and it detaches with the lightest tug**, leaving a hollow thimble. If you have to pull, it's not ready.
- Morning is best (firmer, cooler). Bring a shallow container — they crush easily.
- Watch the thorns and check for the odd insect inside; a quick float-rinse sorts them out.

## Prep
- Best eaten straight off the cane. Beyond that: over yogurt/oatmeal, into pancakes, or a quick fridge jam.
- Freeze on a tray then bag, so they don't clump — great through winter.

## Notes
- Canes are biennial: this year's fruiting canes die back, new ones take over — so a good patch produces year after year.
