Aronia is one of the great under-appreciated urban berries — commonly planted as a landscape shrub, so it's everywhere, and almost nobody picks it. Raw it's ferociously astringent (that's the "choke"), but it's one of the highest-antioxidant fruits going, and it transforms completely once cooked or sweetened.

## Harvest
- Pick when berries are fully **purple-black and slightly soft**, August into September. They hold on the bush for weeks, so there's no rush.
- Whole clusters strip off easily. A frost sweetens them and knocks back the astringency — like rowan, it's worth waiting or freezing the pick overnight.

## Prep
- **Raw:** edible but drying — a nibble is fine, a handful is a pucker. Best mixed into other things.
- **Juice / syrup:** the classic use — deep ruby, tart, great cut with apple or added to sparkling water.
- **Jam & baking:** cook down with sugar (and pectin-rich apple/crabapple) into a dark preserve; or fold into muffins, oatmeal, smoothies.
- **Dried:** dehydrate for a chewy raisin-like snack that mellows the astringency.

## Notes
- Very high in anthocyanins/vitamin C — the health-food "aronia" powders are this exact berry.
