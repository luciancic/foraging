The little lemony clover-lookalike growing in lawns, cracks, and garden edges. A fun, safe, instantly-rewarding nibble — the bright sour hit makes it a favourite for kids and a nice trail snack.

## Harvest
- Pinch leaves, flowers, and young seed pods — all edible and all tangy.
- Grows everywhere; take a little as you pass.

## Prep
- Eat raw as a trail nibble, scatter over salads, or use as a lemony garnish on fish and soups.
- Steep a handful in cold water for a lightly sour "lemonade."

## Notes
_(Nothing to log really — it's everywhere. A good confidence plant.)_
