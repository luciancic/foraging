Ornamental crabapples line streets and parks all over Verdun — two are already on the map (Gaétan-Laberge and rue de Verdun & Beatty). Most are ignored and left to drop, which makes them a reliable, high-yield forage.

## Harvest
- Pick when fully coloured and slightly soft; a good tree gives kilos. Taste one first — some cultivars are mouth-puckeringly tart (great cooked), others pleasant raw.
- They hold on the tree well into fall, sweetening after the first frost.

## Prep
- **Crabapple jelly** is the classic — they're loaded with natural pectin, so jelly sets with no added pectin. Simmer whole, strain, add sugar, set.
- Also great: apple butter, sauce, chutney, or muddled into cider/shrubs. Halve and core the bigger ones; tiny ones just simmer whole and strain.
