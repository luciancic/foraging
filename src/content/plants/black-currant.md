Cassis — the intensely aromatic black berry behind the liqueur and the jam. Escaped garden plants turn up in hedges and along fences; the crush-and-smell test makes it one of the easier berries to confirm.

## Harvest
- Pick when berries are fully **black, glossy and soft**, mid-to-late summer. Ripe ones come off easily; strip whole strings and sort later.
- The flavour is much more assertive than a store raspberry — sharp, musky, "grown-up." A little goes a long way.

## Prep
- **Raw:** edible but tart and resinous — most people prefer them cooked or sweetened.
- **Jam / cassis syrup:** the classic. High in pectin, so they set well.
- **Cordial / infusions:** steep in sugar syrup (or spirit, for a homemade crème de cassis).
- **Leaves:** a few aromatic young leaves make a fragrant tea.

## Notes
- Very high in vitamin C.
