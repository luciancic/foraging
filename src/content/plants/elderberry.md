A two-season plant: fragrant **elderflowers** now (fritters, cordial) and dark **elderberries** in late August–September (syrup, immune tonic). Both need a light touch — the flowers are delicate and the berries must be cooked.

## Harvest
- **Flowers:** snip whole umbels on a dry sunny morning when most florets are open and fragrant; shake out insects, don't wash (rinses off pollen/aroma). Use fresh.
- **Berries:** cut whole clusters when fully purple-black and drooping; strip berries off the stems with a fork (keep stems out — they're the bitter/toxic part).

## Prep
- **Elderflower cordial:** steep flowers with sugar, lemon, and water; strain — a classic summer syrup for spritzers.
- **Elderberry syrup:** simmer de-stemmed berries with water, strain, add honey — the well-known cold/immune syrup. Always cooked.
