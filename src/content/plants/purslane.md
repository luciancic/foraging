A crunchy, lemony succulent growing free in the cracks of the city, and one of the richest plant sources of omega-3 fatty acids. Most people weed it out without knowing it's a delicacy (it's sold in markets in Mexico and the Mediterranean).

## Harvest
- Pinch off the tender stem tips and leaves; it regrows readily from what's left.
- Harvest from clean ground — since it grows at foot level in sidewalks, avoid dog-traffic spots and rinse very well.

## Prep
- **Raw:** whole sprigs in salads — crisp, juicy, lemon-tart. Great with tomato, cucumber, feta.
- **Cooked:** thickens soups and stews slightly (a mild okra-like effect); good sautéed, in stir-fries, or folded into eggs.
- Classic Mexican *verdolagas* — stewed with tomatillo and pork.

## Notes
_(Cleanest patches you trust.)_
