The friendliest street tree to forage — nothing about it is dangerous, and it gives two things: a lovely calming tea from the flowers, and a mild salad green from the spring leaves.

## Harvest
- **Flowers:** pick with their papery bract in early summer, on a dry day, just as they open. Dry in a single layer out of sun for tea.
- **Leaves:** the tender, translucent young spring leaves are the salad ones; older leaves get tough.

## Prep
- **Linden flower tea (tilleul):** steep a small handful of dried flowers in just-off-boil water 5 min. Mild, honeyed, faintly floral — a traditional evening/calming tea.
- Young leaves go straight into salads or sandwiches — soft and mild, a good bulk green.
