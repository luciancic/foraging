The single best "hidden fruit" of the July city. Mulberries are planted as street and yard trees all over, and most people never notice them until the sidewalk underneath turns purple.

## Harvest
- Ripe fruit is **soft and drops at a touch**. Lay a sheet or open umbrella under a branch and shake — ripe berries rain down, unripe ones stay put.
- Fruit ripens unevenly over 2–3 weeks, so the same tree keeps giving. Beat the birds in the morning.
- They **stain everything** — fingers, clothes, car roofs. Wear old clothes; park elsewhere.

## Prep
- Eat fresh by the handful. They're delicate and don't keep — refrigerate and use within a day or two.
- Freeze on a tray then bag for smoothies / baking.
- Slightly low in acidity, so cook with a squeeze of lemon for jam or to brighten a pie. Great in muffins, over yogurt, or as a quick compote.
